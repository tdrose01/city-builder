# Product Guidelines - City Slacker

## Tone and Voice
- **Electric & Sophisticated:** The game messaging should be high-energy and celebratory during wins, balanced by a modern, clean urban sophistication.
- **Urban Architect Persona:** Address the player as an "Urban Architect" to instill a sense of ownership over the skyline.

## Visual Feedback Principles
- **Dopamine-Driven Celebration:** Use explosive visuals, particle bursts, and high-impact animations for dice rolls and landmark upgrades to maximize immediate satisfaction.
- **Architectural Precision:** Animations for structural evolution should be sleek and deliberate, emphasizing growth and quality construction.
- **Ambient Immersion:** Prioritize environmental feedback, such as the city backdrop gradually illuminating as landmarks are upgraded.

## UI/UX Standards
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Keep the main viewport clean and focused on the board loop. Secondary information (sticker sets, detailed metrics) should be tucked into sleek, expandable cards or overlays.
- **Visual Abstraction:** Prioritize high-contrast badges, progress bars, and icons over dense text blocks to ensure readability on various screen sizes.
- **Contextual Callouts:** Use brief, localized HUD updates (e.g., "Set Complete!", "Doubles!") near the action point to minimize eye travel.
- **Uniform Sizing:** All board tiles should be the same size (uniform grid) for consistent visual hierarchy and clean appearance.
- **Generous Spacing:** Tiles should have substantial breathing room (12-20px gaps) to prevent visual congestion.
- **Simplified Labels:** Tile labels should show only essential information (type names) in a single line for instant recognition.

## Branding & Identity
- **Brand Pillar: "Fortune Favors the Bold":** Messaging should lean into the high-roller, high-reward nature of the dice economy.
- **Interaction Aesthetic: "Neon Harbor":**
    - **Luminous UI:** All primary actions and updated tiles should feature glowing hover states or light-up animations.
    - **Glassmorphism:** Use frosted glass backgrounds and sharp, illuminated borders for all panels to reinforce the modern urban feel.
    - **3D Depth:** Employ layered shadows and subtle 3D tilts for cards and board tiles to create a tactile, high-end feel.

## Board Design Principles (Updated 2026-01-20)
- **Clarity Over Density:** Reduced tile count (20 vs 40) prioritizes readability and reduces cognitive load.
- **Uniform Grid System:** 6x6 grid with equal-sized tiles creates consistent, predictable visual rhythm.
- **Type-First Labels:** Tile labels show only the type (FUNDS, HEIST, etc.) for instant recognition without reading detailed names.
- **Integrated Status:** Board status appears inline in the center area, keeping all information within the board viewport.
- **Responsive Spacing:** Gaps scale appropriately (12-20px) to maintain clean appearance across different screen sizes.
- **Equal Hierarchy:** All tiles (including corners) have identical sizing to emphasize gameplay over visual decoration.
