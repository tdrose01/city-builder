# Specification - Gameplay Balance and Content Expansion

## Overview
This track tunes the session pacing to the 60-120s target and expands content with a second city layout and unique tile set variants. It also improves sticker album progression hints so players can see the meta loop clearly during short sessions.

## Objectives
- **Gameplay Balance:** Adjust roll probabilities, milestone rewards, and mission/event pacing to hit the 60-120s session goal.
- **Content Expansion:** Define and implement a second city layout with distinct tile labels and visuals.
- **Sticker Hints:** Surface clearer progression hints for sets and currencies without adding scroll.

## Requirements
- Preserve the no-scroll, single-screen layout.
- Maintain the Neon Harbor visual style.
- Do not regress core loop clarity (roll -> move -> resolve -> upgrade).
- Updates must be reflected in docs and economy CSVs where applicable.

## Success Criteria
- Average sessions land in the 60-120s target range with 8-12 rolls.
- Event milestones and missions complete at a steady cadence across a session.
- Second city is selectable or reachable with unique tiles and readable labels.
- Sticker album hints make progress and currency flow obvious within the HUD.
