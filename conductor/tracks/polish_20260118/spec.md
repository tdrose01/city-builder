# Specification - Implement Enhanced Tile Effects and Gameplay Polish

## Overview
This track focuses on the visual and interactive "juice" of the City Slacker board loop. Based on the PRD's follow-up work, we need to transition from functional mechanics to a polished "Neon Harbor" experience.

## Objectives
- **Unique Tile Feedback:** Wire specific visual effects (glows, icons, or HUD callouts) for each tile type landing.
- **Enhanced Upgrade Feedback:** Add particle bursts or text pops when a landmark is upgraded.
- **3D Dice Polish:** Add contact shadows or floor blur to the 3D dice in the board center to improve depth.
- **HUD Reinforcement:** Highlight dice streaks and currency changes with clearer visual badges.

## Requirements
- Effects must not interfere with the "no-scroll" layout.
- Visuals must adhere to the "Neon Harbor" (high-contrast, luminous) aesthetic.
- Performance must remain high for web-based play.

## Success Criteria
- Landing on a "Shield" tile triggers a distinct visual feedback compared to a "Funds" tile.
- Upgrading a landmark triggers a noticeable "celebration" effect at the tile location.
- 3D dice feel "grounded" on the board rather than floating.
