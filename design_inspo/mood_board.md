# City Stacker Mood Board

## Aesthetic: "Neon-Noir Art Deco"
**Keywords**: Verticality, Glass, Neon, Grit, Luxury vs. Decay.

## Color Palette
- **Neon Blue**: `#00f3ff` (Holograms, UI, High-tech)
- **Neon Pink**: `#d946ef` (Warning lights, "Sin City" vibes)
- **Urban Black**: `#0f172a` (Shadows, The Void)
- **Gold**: `#fbbf24` (Wealth, VIP floors)

## Layout Inspiration
- **Monopoly Go**: Center board, floating UI elements.
- **Cyberpunk 2077 Menu**: Glitch effects, red/black contrast.
- **SimCity 2000**: Isometric view (for the board).

## UI Components
- **Buttons**: Glass-morphism with neon borders.
- **Modals**: Dark overlays with backdrop blur.
- **Typography**: `Inter` for readability, `Orbitron` or similar for headers.
